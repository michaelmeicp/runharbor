# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience. Critique code and ideas without attacking people.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sharing private information, impersonation, and spam are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through an available maintainer contact channel. Do not repeat private information in a public issue. A dedicated private conduct-reporting address remains to be configured before the public launch.
